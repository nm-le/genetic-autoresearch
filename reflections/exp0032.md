## Decision
Depth=10 wins despite fewer steps. The earlier failure at depth=9 (exp0010,
val=1.009) was the *combination* of bigger model and the slower batch=2**19
regime. With batch=2**18 giving us more updates, depth=10 has enough steps
to converge. Promote → new elite/0.

## Revisit
- The matrix_lr optimum likely shifts at depth=10 (model_dim went 512→640,
  so dmodel_lr_scale changes from 1.225 to 1.095 — Adam params get less
  amplification). May want lr higher.
- Try depth=12 if VRAM allows (currently 66 GB, headroom 13 GB).
- Try DEPTH=10 + warmup (haven't tested that combo at this depth).
