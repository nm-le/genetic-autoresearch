## Decision
Way too undertrained. The bigger model needs more tokens to make up for its
extra capacity, but the time budget caps total compute. The compute-step
trade-off lost: -33% steps wasn't compensated by +61% params.

## Revisit
For deeper/bigger architectures to win, need either: (a) DEPTH=9 with smaller
ASPECT_RATIO so model_dim stays low, or (b) reduce DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE to fit
larger models, or (c) DEPTH=10+ where the architectural win is large enough.
Skip incremental DEPTH tweaks; they're undertrained at this budget.
