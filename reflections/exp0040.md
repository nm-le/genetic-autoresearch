# exp0040 — ASPECT_RATIO 64 → 72 (CRASH: OOM)

- parent1: 30b0cc5 (elite/3 = exp0037, depth=10, lr=0.0275, warmup,
  batch=2**18)
- parent2: -
- operator: mutate
- what changed: `ASPECT_RATIO = 64 → 72`. With depth=10, base_dim=720
  rounds up to model_dim=768 (6 heads × 128).

## Outcome
Crashed with `torch.OutOfMemoryError`. Allocated 74.75 GiB / 79.19 GiB
total before OOM at the lm_head logits buffer (262144 × 8192).

## Stats before crash
- params: 114.8M
- mode: depth=10, model_dim=768, 6 heads
- step 0 logged loss only (compile + first fwd ate the budget)

## Decision
Won't fit at this depth with batch=2**18 + DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE=128. To
explore wider models at depth=10, must lower DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE (which
adds grad accumulation overhead) or reduce TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE (which we
saw hurts at 2**17). Skip this combo.

## Revisit
- Don't try ASPECT_RATIO ≥ 72 at depth=10 without lowering DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE.
- Try ASPECT_RATIO=72 at depth=8 instead (model_dim=576 → rounds to 640,
  same as depth=10 base; tests width-vs-depth at fixed model_dim).
- Or try ASPECT_RATIO=64 with DEPTH=12 — but DEPTH=12 may also OOM.

exp0010 (depth=9 OOM-adjacent at batch=2**19, val=1.009). Same axis,
same outcome: too big for the budget.
