# exp0020 — TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE 2**19 → 2**18 (BIG WIN)

- parent1: a546b54 (elite/1, lr=0.05, batch=524K)
- parent2: -
- operator: mutate
- what changed: `TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE = 2**19 (524288)` → `2**18 (262144)`.
  Halves tokens per optimizer step. With DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE=128 and seq=2048,
  this reduces grad_accum_steps from 2 to 1 — every fwdbwd is now an
  optimizer step.

## Decision
Far past the 0.000150 promotion margin. Smaller batches give us 2× more
updates with same total compute, and at this scale the extra optimizer
steps dominate the noise penalty. Strong promote → new elite/0 (best).

## Revisit
This is the most impactful axis seen so far. Try going further:
- TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE=2**17 (131K) → 4× more steps, but DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE=128
  would force grad_accum=0.5 which is illegal — must lower DEVICE_BATCH_SIZE
  to 64 to make 2**17 work (grad_accum=1).
- Or keep 2**18 and re-explore other axes (matrix_lr, etc.) since
  optima may shift with smaller batch.
