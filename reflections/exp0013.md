## Decision
The combination is *worse than either parent*. At lr=0.045 warmup was a no-op
(exp0003 vs exp0004 both ~0.99571). At lr=0.0475 warmup costs ~0.0014. The
higher peak LR amplifies whatever phase mismatch warmup introduces (e.g. the
ramp wastes Muon momentum buildup or interacts badly with the cooldown
window). Warmup is harmful at the matrix_lr optimum. Don't promote.

## Revisit
Warmup is a dead axis at this configuration. Remove it from the search.
